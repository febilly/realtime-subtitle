# Open Source Licenses and Acknowledgements

This project stands on the shoulders of giants. Below is the list of open-source libraries and resources used in this project, along with their verified licenses:

- **[PySide6 / Qt6](https://www.qt.io/)** — Used for the native subtitle overlay window. Licensed under the [LGPLv3 License](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html).
- **[pyopenvr](https://github.com/cmbruns/pyopenvr)** — Python bindings used for the SteamVR subtitle overlay. Licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://github.com/cmbruns/pyopenvr/blob/master/LICENSE). It ships Valve's **[OpenVR](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/openvr)** runtime (`libopenvr_api_64.dll`, bundled into the executable), licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/openvr/blob/master/LICENSE).
- **[Lucide Icons](https://lucide.dev/)** — Used for modern line icons in the UI and overlay window. Licensed under the [ISC License](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **[kuromoji.js](https://github.com/takuyaa/kuromoji.js)** — Used for Japanese tokenization (furigana) in the browser and, via the main window, for the native subtitle overlay. Licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/takuyaa/kuromoji.js/blob/master/LICENSE-2.0.txt).
- **mecab-ipadic** — The Japanese dictionary bundled with kuromoji.js. Licensed under the [IPADIC license](static/kuromoji/dict/LICENSE) (NAIST / ICOT).
- **[libopus](https://opus-codec.org/)** — Used for Opus audio encoding (bundled `opus.dll`). Licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://opus-codec.org/license/).
- **[pywebview](https://pywebview.flowrl.com/)** — Used to build the main GUI window. Licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://github.com/r0xsh/pywebview/blob/master/LICENSE).
- **[soundcard](https://github.com/bastibe/SoundCard)** — Used for system loopback and microphone audio capture. Licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://github.com/bastibe/SoundCard/blob/master/LICENSE).
- **[websockets](https://github.com/python-websockets/websockets)** — Used for fast, real-time message broadcasting. Licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://github.com/python-websockets/websockets/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **[numpy](https://numpy.org/)** — Used for high-performance audio signal operations. Licensed under the [BSD 3-Clause License](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/license.html).
- **[ten-vad](https://github.com/TEN-framework/ten-vad)** — Used for voice activity detection during silent periods. Licensed under the [Apache License 2.0 with additional Agora non-compete conditions](https://github.com/TEN-framework/ten-vad/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **[ONNX Runtime](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime)** — Runs the on-device VAD model. Licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **[gguf](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/master/gguf-py)** — Reads GGUF model files for the on-device models. Licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE). It pulls in **[PyYAML](https://github.com/yaml/pyyaml)** ([MIT License](https://github.com/yaml/pyyaml/blob/main/LICENSE)) and **[tqdm](https://github.com/tqdm/tqdm)** ([MPL-2.0 and MIT](https://github.com/tqdm/tqdm/blob/master/LICENCE)), which are bundled as well.
- **[llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp)** — Runs the on-device speech recognition (audio encoder via libmtmd, and decoder) and translation models. Not bundled: its Vulkan DLLs are downloaded on first use of local mode, from a [Hugging Face copy](https://huggingface.co/febilly/realtime-subtitle-local-runtime) of the official release, with GitHub as the fallback. Licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/LICENSE). The download includes the LLVM OpenMP runtime (`libomp.dll`), licensed under [Apache-2.0 with LLVM Exceptions](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project/blob/main/openmp/LICENSE.TXT).
- **[Qwen3-ASR](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR)** by the Qwen team — The on-device speech recognition model. Not bundled: downloaded on first use of local mode. Licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR/blob/main/LICENSE). The 1.7B GGUF conversion comes from [mradermacher](https://huggingface.co/mradermacher/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-GGUF), the 0.6B one from [ggml-org](https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B-GGUF).
- **[Hy-MT2](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hy-MT2-1.8B)** by Tencent — Base model of the on-device translation model ([fine-tuned GGUF](https://huggingface.co/febilly/Hy-MT2-1.8B-StreamRevise-v4-GGUF)). Not bundled: downloaded on first use of local mode. Licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
- **[Silero VAD](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad)** — The on-device voice activity detection model. Not bundled: downloaded on first use of local mode. Licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad/blob/master/LICENSE).
- **[Qwen3-ASR-GGUF](https://github.com/HaujetZhao/Qwen3-ASR-GGUF)** by HaujetZhao, by way of **[Yakutan](https://github.com/KKTIME2024/Yakutan)** ([MIT License](https://github.com/KKTIME2024/Yakutan/blob/main/LICENSE)) and **[LiveTranslate](https://github.com/TheDeathDragon/LiveTranslate)** ([MIT License](https://github.com/TheDeathDragon/LiveTranslate/blob/main/LICENSE)) — The approach of splitting Qwen3-ASR into an audio encoder and a llama.cpp decoder, and the llama.cpp bindings in `local_inference/vendor/qwen_asr_gguf`.
- **[python-osc](https://github.com/attwad/python-osc)** — Used for VRChat OSC integration. Licensed under the [Unlicense](https://github.com/attwad/python-osc/blob/master/LICENSE.txt).
- **[vrchat_oscquery](https://github.com/theepicsnail/vrchat_oscquery)** — Used for VRChat OSCQuery integration. Licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/theepicsnail/vrchat_oscquery/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **[aiohttp](https://github.com/aio-libs/aiohttp)** — Used for the local asynchronous web server. Licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/aio-libs/aiohttp/blob/master/LICENSE.txt).
- **[requests](https://github.com/psf/requests)** — Used for synchronous HTTP requests. Licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://github.com/psf/requests/blob/main/LICENSE).
